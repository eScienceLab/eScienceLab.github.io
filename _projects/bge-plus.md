[Biodiversity Genomics Europe plus](https://biodiversitygenomics.eu/) (BGE+) is a Horizon Europe project funded under [HORIZON-CL6-2025-01-BIODIV-03](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-CL6-2025-01-BIODIV-03), and coordinated by Naturalis Biodiversity Center. It builds on the previous [Biodiversity Genomics Europe project](/projects/biodiversitygenomics/) to scale up the production and use of genomic evidence for taxonomy, biodiversity monitoring and policy.

BGE+ brings together the European communities around [**iBOL Europe**](https://iboleurope.org/) (International Barcode of Life) for DNA barcoding, [**ERGA**](https://www.erga-biodiversity.eu/) (European Reference Genome Atlas) and [**CETAF**](https://cetaf.org/) (Consortium of European Taxonomic Facilities). This project aims to turn distributed biodiversity genomics activities into a more coherent European system, with shared protocols, interoperable data and sustainable services.

## Objectives

1. Broaden participation and capacity for biodiversity genomics across Europe.
2. Remove technical barriers to producing and analysing DNA barcodes and whole-genome sequence data.
3. Increase the uptake of genomic evidence in biodiversity monitoring, environmental management and policy.
4. Establish the foundations for a sustainable distributed European biodiversity genomics system.

The project combines cascade funding and training with the development of laboratory protocols, bioinformatics tools and FAIR data infrastructure. It will also develop policy-oriented roadmaps for DNA barcoding, metabarcoding and whole-genome approaches, while investigating how biodiversity genomics services could become part of existing European Research Infrastructures.

## FAIR data and RO-Crate

BGE+ follows an **open science, open data and open source** approach. Sequence data will be deposited in established repositories such as ENA and BOLD, while biodiversity observations and other outputs will use appropriate FAIR repositories and community standards.

[RO-Crate](/products/researchobject/) provides an important part of the project's FAIR Digital Object (FDO) and provenance approach. BGE+ will use RO-Crate to package tabular data and associated metadata, describe computational analyses and workflow runs, and connect datasets, workflows, provenance and publications.

RO-Crate is used for:

* laboratory metadata from distributed metabarcoding experiments will be captured as FAIR RO-Crates
* sequence repositories and biodiversity infrastructures will be aligned with FAIR Digital Object approaches including RO-Crate and FAIR Signposting
* workflows and datasets will use RO-Crate to capture rich metadata, provenance and citation information
* RO-Crate editor e.g. Crate-O to be integrated with the ARPHA scholarly publishing system for publisher [Pensoft](https://pensoft.net/)
* a generalised **Publication RO-Crate profile** will be developed for linking articles with biodiversity repositories and detailed data provenance
* an updated RO-Crate profile for metabarcoding will document analytical steps so that archived data can be audited, reprocessed and reused.

## eScience Lab involvement

The eScience Lab contributes its expertise in RO-Crate, FAIR provenance and research workflows. 

* WP4: Improving laboratory protocols and approaches (lead: NHMUK)
  - Task T4.5: Laboratory Intercalibration Framework for DNA Metabarcoding (lead: CIBIO)
  - Deliverable D4.4: Intercalibration Protocol Package (lead: CIBIO)

* WP5: Improving bioinformatic tools and services (lead: EMBL)
  - Task T5.4: Scaling up interoperability between sequence repositories and biodiversity infrastructures (lead: EMBL-EBI)
  - Deliverable D5.3: Integrated system of sequence and biodiversity infrastructures (lead: EMBL)
  - Task T5.5: Streamlining publication with FAIR access to biodiversity genomics data (lead: Pensoft)
  - Milestone MS5.6: Pilot implementation of RO-Crate system for data and metadata associated with journal articles (lead: Pensoft)
  - Deliverable D5.6: Tools and workflows for scholarly publishing of barcoding and reference genome biodiversity data (lead: Pensoft)
  - Task T5.7: Pilot framework for future-proofing metabarcoding-based monitoring (lead: EMBL-EBI)
  - Deliverable D5.7: Validation and calibration of DNA metabarcoding analysis framework (lead: EMBL)
